Frequently asked

Fair questions. Straight answers.

What is a rotating savings circle?+

A group of people who trust each other agree to pay a set amount on a regular schedule. Each turn, one member takes the whole pot, until everyone has had theirs. It is a long standing way to save together, known as Ajó and Esusu in West Africa, Susu in the Caribbean, and by other names elsewhere.

Is my money safe?+

Your contributions are held by a regulated payments partner and protected even if àjọkẹ́ itself ran into trouble, so your money is returned to you rather than being at risk. Every contribution and payout is logged so the circle always adds up.

What happens if someone does not pay?+

A missed contribution is flagged the day after the deadline and counts against that member's Reputation Score. If a payout comes up short, the shortfall is recorded against the person who missed and repaid to whoever was affected first at the next turn. àjọkẹ́ keeps a formal record to support recovery, but it does not guarantee or cover a missed contribution itself, so choose your circle with people you trust.

Who can join?+

Anyone in the UK who passes identity verification. You can start your own circle, join one by invite, or find an open circle looking for members.

Is there a fee?+

A small fee of 0.3 percent is applied once, when a payout is made. There is no second fee if arrears are later recovered. A free plan covers running one circle and joining another at the same time.
